Sweet Dreams




— What is SweetDreams? —

SweetDreams is an application which gradually lowers the volume of YouTube videos to 0 over a set period of time. This application is perfect for users who enjoy falling asleep with noise but don't want to fiddle around with the volume of their devices.

— How do I use SweetDreams? —

1. Paste the URL of a YouTube video in the input field
2. Designate when you want the volume to reach 0. You can do this by either setting a target time, or by selecting the 5 minute, 10 minute, 15 minute, etc. options.
3. Sweet dreams!

— What operating systems does SweetDreams support? —

Currently SweetDreams works on all operating systems except macOS and iOS.
